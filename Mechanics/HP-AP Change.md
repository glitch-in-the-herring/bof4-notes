HP-AP change works a bit differently from Breath of Fire III as the space is shared for both HP and AP. Instead of changing the HP and AP in the same frame, they must be changed in different frames. The total HP-AP change is calculated even if the changed stats were different. For example, if a character uses Rest and recovers 259 HP and 25 AP from it, then the total HP-AP change is 284, even though combining HP and AP make no sense.
